# Hi, I'm Daniel 👋

I'm **Daniel Dos Santos Da Silva**, a Computer Science student at Universidade Estácio de Sá, in Brazil, learning robotics the hands-on way: by building complete robots, from the first CAD sketch to a working ROS 2 simulation. My goal is to work as a **robotics software engineer**, in Brazil or abroad.

What I enjoy most is the whole pipeline: deciding how a robot should be built, designing parts that can really be printed and assembled, and writing the software that makes it move. Hardware costs money, so for now my robots live in simulation, but every one of them is designed to be built for real.

## What I'm working toward

- 🦾 **Manipulation**: kinematics, motion planning with MoveIt 2, grasping
- 🚗 **Mobile robots**: control, navigation and SLAM with Nav2
- 🧠 **Robot learning**: teleoperation and imitation learning on low-cost arms
- 🕷️ **Legged robots**: kinematics and gait on a hexapod
- 📈 **Control**: from PID to model predictive control

## How I work

- **One model for simulation and fabrication.** Parts are parametric code CAD with ISO hardware, datasheet hole patterns and stress checks, and the same model generates the URDF.
- **Nothing taken on trust.** Every stage is checked against a datasheet, a hand calculation or the CAD itself, and I always say what is simulated and what is real.
- **Decisions are documented**: the alternatives and why I chose one, not just the result.
- **AI as a pair programmer.** I use it to move faster; the design decisions and the verification are mine.
